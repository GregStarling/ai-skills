# Source ledger

Design Director's language and contracts are original. This ledger records
ideas studied, not copied command systems or upstream runtime code.

## Human-factors laws and principles

The design principles reference synthesizes Hick's Law, Fitts's Law, Jakob's
Law, the laws of Proximity, Pragnanz, and Similarity, Miller's Law, the Doherty
Threshold, the Von Restorff Effect, Uniform Connectedness, Tesler's Law,
Postel's Law, Parkinson's Law, Occam's Razor, the Pareto Principle, minimizing
target distance, the Serial Position Effect, the Peak-End Rule, and the
Zeigarnik Effect.

- Adopted: their durable implications for choice, acquisition, familiarity,
  grouping, memory, feedback, emphasis, progress, recovery, simplicity, and
  prioritization.
- Adapted: user-directed synthesis into original directives, design-system
  implications, misuse guardrails, and verification questions. The reference
  records conceptual lineage for each principle.
- Rejected: treating a named law as universal proof, product-specific evidence,
  a mandatory floor clause, or authority for manipulative simplification.

## Impeccable

Source: [pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), Apache-2.0,
copyright 2025 Paul Bakaus.

- Adopted: persistent product context, explicit craft floor, real-state
  hardening, and rendered iteration.
- Adapted: product/visual authority separation and bounded polish into evidence,
  constitution, practice-profile, and proof contracts.
- Rejected: category as style authority, blanket taste bans, command catalog,
  detector/runtime, browser extension, and provider packaging.

## UI/UX Pro Max

Source: [nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill),
MIT, copyright 2024 Next Level Builder.

- Adopted: cross-platform craft checklists and explicit responsive/accessibility
  verification.
- Adapted: searchable guidance into optional supporting practice input.
- Rejected: product-category matching, ranked style/color/font recipes, and
  catalog results as product authority.

## Taste Skill

Source: [Leonxlnx/taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill), MIT,
copyright 2026 Leonxlnx.

- Adopted: deliberate hierarchy, typography, spacing, motion, and non-generic
  composition as review prompts.
- Adapted: taste assertions into evidence-challenged hypotheses.
- Rejected: universal stylistic bans and “anti-slop” preference as authority.

## Emil Kowalski's skills

Source: [emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills), MIT,
copyright 2026 Emil Kowalski.

- Adopted: motion must have a reason; choose curve, duration, property, gesture,
  interruption, and platform behavior intentionally.
- Adapted: specialist motion reviews into the interaction/motion profile and
  independent critic evidence.
- Rejected: library-specific recipes as universal product clauses.

## Intent

Source: [ghaida/intent](https://github.com/ghaida/intent), CC0-1.0.

- Adopted: evidence-led problem framing, user journeys, content/IA,
  accessibility, ethics, measurement, resilience, and engineering handoff.
- Adapted: specialist routing into compact practice profiles selected by work
  kind, surface mode, risk, and evidence.
- Rejected: a second agent/director hierarchy and routine human checkpoints.

## Perception-First Design

Source: [skovalik/perception-first-design](https://github.com/skovalik/perception-first-design),
framework text CC BY-SA 4.0; Perception-First Design™ is a trademark of Stefan
Kovalik / Aurochs. Practice application is permitted by its published exemption.

- Adopted as practice, in original language: diagnose what users can
  perceive, process, trust, and act on; resolve upstream blocking failures before
  downstream polish.
- Adapted: none of its framework prose, branded layer names, calibration corpus,
  or skill text.
- Rejected: redistribution or modification of CC BY-SA framework prose and use
  of its trademark as product/feature branding.
