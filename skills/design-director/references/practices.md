# Practice profiles

Select by work kind, surface mode, risk, maturity, and evidence. Product
category never chooses a visual style.

Each profile selects its relevant defaults from
[principles.md](principles.md). A host runtime may project the deterministic
union for all selected profiles into a task. Apply that union in canonical
order, without treating profile selection as product evidence or a new
constitution clause.

## Strategy and UX

Define the user, job, use context, decision path, desired outcomes,
countermetrics, ethical boundaries, and recovery. Trace the path from entry to
outcome and the important failure states.

## Visual direction

Separate product truth from visual authority. For refinement and ordinary
extension, preserve established identity. For new surfaces or visual
replacement, articulate a product-specific direction from audience, content,
platform, and outcomes. Avoid generic template composition and unsupported
anti-pattern bans.

## Interaction and motion

Specify cause, response, state transition, timing intent, interruption,
keyboard/focus behavior, reduced-motion behavior, and recovery. Add motion only
when it improves continuity, causality, orientation, feedback, or expression.

## Content and information architecture

Use honest product content. Define hierarchy, labels, navigation, disclosure,
content ranges, and scanning/reading behavior. Treat voice and microcopy as
product behavior when they affect comprehension or choice.

## Redesign archaeology

Inspect existing product docs, tokens, components, styles, assets, routes, and
neighboring flows before replacing anything. Missing documentation does not
make an implemented product greenfield. Record what evidence says to preserve,
retire, or reinterpret.

## Perception and evaluation

Evaluate what users can notice, group, comprehend, trust, and act on before
debating surface taste. Diagnose from rendered evidence and user context. Fix
blocking perceptual/decision failures before downstream polish.

## Exploration

Skip alternatives for refinements and ordinary extensions unless evidence
exposes a real direction conflict. Otherwise explore at most three materially
different directions. Name each divergence axis and use truthful product
content. Choose autonomously; if evidence ties, choose reversibility and mark
the affected clause experimental.
